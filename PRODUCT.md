# Moonlit Boat Ride

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Three.js, as requested. Assumption for this first build: a small Vite application with no UI framework.

## Users

People exploring a quiet, interactive night scene from a third-person view.

## Product Purpose

Let someone steer an empty boat around a moonlit lake and enjoy the atmosphere.

## Operating Context

Full-screen browser experience controlled with a keyboard. The scene includes water, moonlight reflections, wind, and trees around the shore.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Keyboard steering and movement.
- Third-person camera following the boat.
- Empty, visible boat.
- Moonlight reflecting on animated water.
- Subtle wind movement in shoreline trees and the boat.
- References supplied by the user: three attached images and four links.

## Evidence on Hand

The three attached images show low moons, a bright water reflection, and a solitary wooden boat in dark blue night scenes. The linked pages were inaccessible to the browsing tool during the initial pass.

## Product Principles

- Put the scene ahead of interface chrome.
- Keep the boat readable and easy to steer.
- Use restrained motion to sustain a peaceful mood.
